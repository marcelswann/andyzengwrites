ANDY QIFENG ZENG

Andy is a writer from Beijing living in New York City and an MFA student in fiction at Columbia University. His work appears in or is forthcoming in Wigleaf, Bending Genres, Identity Theory, Carmen et Error, and elsewhere. His favorite writer is Marcel Proust, and his favorite book is Humboldt’s Gift. Before turning to fiction, he wrote for the NYU Stern Center for Business & Human Rights and edited the Harvard Review of Philosophy and Harvard Tech Review. He holds a BA in Philosophy from Harvard and an MMSc from Tsinghua University, where he was a Schwarzman Scholar.
