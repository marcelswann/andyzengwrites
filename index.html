ANDY QIFENG ZENG

Andy is a writer and RedNote content creator from Beijing living in New York. He is currently pursuing an MFA in Fiction at Columbia University.

His work appears in Wigleaf, Bending Genres, Identity Theory, Carmen et Error, and elsewhere. His favorite writer is Marcel Proust, and his favorite novel is Humboldt’s Gift. He doesn't read enough poetry, but his favorite poet is 李煜.

Before turning to fiction, he wrote for the NYU Stern Center for Business & Human Rights and edited the Harvard Review of Philosophy and Harvard Tech Review.
